Heat Exchangers
Balancing thermal energy
Heat Exchangers transfer heat between two fluid conversions. Side A and side B each have an input tank and an output tank. The machine runs only when one registered conversion releases the same amount of heat that the other conversion absorbs. Pipe fluid into input ports. Each output port automatically pushes converted fluid into an adjacent handler or IE pipe network with an accepting destination, and remains available for external extraction.
Conversions are supplied by datapacks or recipe-script integrations; there is deliberately no universal default conversion. Right-click any formed block with the main hand to open the four-tank interface. Sneak-right-click remains available for pipes and tools. The side arrows clear both tanks and filters on that side, the center button clears all four tanks, and each filter button can lock a non-empty tank to its current fluid.
<np><&small>The Small Heat Exchanger is a straight line of Radiator, two Iron Sheetmetal blocks, and a second Radiator. Strike either end Radiator with an Engineer's Hammer to form it. Each Radiator is one fluid side: its top face is the input and its bottom face is the output. It requires 2 Iron Sheetmetal blocks and 2 Radiators.
<np><&medium>The Medium Heat Exchanger is a solid 5x2x2 structure. One 2x2 end wall is Radiators and the other 16 blocks are Iron Sheetmetal. Strike either upper Radiator on that end wall with an Engineer's Hammer; the structure may form mirrored. Side A inputs through the top of one upper Radiator and outputs through the underside of the diagonally opposite lower Radiator. Side B uses the adjacent Sheetmetal column: its lower downward face is the input and its diagonally opposite upper upward face is the output. It requires 16 Iron Sheetmetal blocks and 4 Radiators.
<np><&large>The Large Heat Exchanger is a solid 3x3x7 structure with a 3x3 Radiator end wall and a five-block Radiator core through the middle. Strike the center Radiator of the end wall with an Engineer's Hammer. Side A uses the top-center face of that Radiator wall as input and its bottom-center underside as output. Side B inputs through the underside of the bottom-center Sheetmetal block two blocks inward from the Radiator wall, and outputs through the top-center Sheetmetal block one block inward from the opposite end. It requires 49 Iron Sheetmetal blocks and 14 Radiators.
